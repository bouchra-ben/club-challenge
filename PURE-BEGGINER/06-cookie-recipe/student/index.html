Cosmic Cookies 🍪

The easiest chocolate-chip cookies in this galaxy.

⏰ 25 minutes | 🍪 12 cookies | ⭐ Super easy

What you'll need

  • 1 cup of flour
  • 1/2 cup of butter
  • 1/2 cup of sugar
  • 1 egg
  • Lots of chocolate chips !

Let's bake!

  1. Mix the butter and sugar.
  2. Add the egg and flour.
  3. Stir in chocolate chips>
  4. Bake for 12 minutes at 180°C.

    5. Grandma's secret: Save one cookie for yourself. Or five.
